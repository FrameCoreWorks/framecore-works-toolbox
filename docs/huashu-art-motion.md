## Use

Ask for an animation in an art style or an explainer grammar (Kurzgesagt-like, whiteboard).

## Watch out

Skill text is in Chinese; do not reuse its showcase characters or likenesses.

## Full removal

Remove it in Customize > Plugins or with `claude plugin uninstall huashu-art-motion@framecore-works-toolbox`.
